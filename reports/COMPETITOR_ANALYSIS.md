### 3.1 Algorithmic Implications in Google Maps Local 3-Pack
Google's local ranking algorithm operates primarily on three pillars: **Relevance**, **Distance (Proximity)**, and **Prominence**.
- **The Proximity Paradox**: B★STAR and Green Trends are only 300 meters apart on the exact same road (TC Palya Main Road). Because both businesses share identical geographical proximity to searchers in Anandapura/Battarahalli, Google's algorithm relies almost entirely on **Prominence** to break ties.
- **Prominence Calculation**: Google Prominence incorporates total review count, average star rating, review velocity (new reviews per month), and sentiment depth (how many reviews mention "keratin", "haircut", "facial", or specific stylists).
- **The Result**: With 4,000+ reviews at 4.8★, Green Trends commands an overwhelming prominence score that anchors it at #1 for generic queries like "salon in tc palya", "best hair salon near me", and "beauty parlour battarahalli".
