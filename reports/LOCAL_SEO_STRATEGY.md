### 2.4 Sitewide Schema Cleanup & Anti-Duplication SOP

The baseline audit revealed a critical violation: in `src/app/layout.tsx`, an `FAQPage` schema containing 6 general homepage questions is rendered sitewide across every single page. This causes severe search penalties because Google requires `FAQPage` markup to represent content that is physically displayed on that specific URL.

- Hyper-local environmental challenges unique to East Bengaluru (e.g. borewell water TDS exceeding 800 PPM, dust from KR Puram flyover / Old Madras Road transit, high monsoon humidity).
