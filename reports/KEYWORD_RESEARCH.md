## 5. Search Intent Gap Analysis & Content Recommendations

### 5.1 Gaps on Existing URLs
- **Homepage (`/`)**: Currently tries to rank for all salon terms simultaneously. Should be focused strictly on `Best Unisex Salon in TC Palya Main Road, Battarahalli` and brand terms.
- **Service Category Pages (`/services/*`)**:
  - Missing pricing FAQs at the bottom of each category page answering exact cost queries (e.g., "How much does a Balayage cost at B★STAR Salon?").
  - Lack comparison content (e.g., "Keratin vs. Hair Botox: Which is Right for Your Hair?").
- **Local Landing Page (`/salons/tc-palya`)**:
  - Performs well for TC Palya, but misses traffic from adjacent neighborhoods (Anandapura, Kalkere, Bhattarahalli).

### 5.2 Recommended New URL Expansion Roadmap
To capture high-intent non-brand queries currently lost to competitors, the following content clusters should be produced:

1. **Dedicated Treatment Spotlight Pages**:
   - `/services/hair/keratin-treatment-bangalore`
   - `/services/hair/balayage-hair-color-bangalore`
   - `/services/skin/hydrafacial-bangalore`
2. **Neighborhood Cluster Landing Pages**:
   - `/salons/battarahalli` (targeting Battarahalli residential catchment)
   - `/salons/anandapura` (targeting Anandapura Circle footfall)
   - `/salons/kr-puram` (capturing high-volume KR Puram search traffic)
3. **Price Guide & Comparison Blog Guides**:
   - `/blog/hair-keratin-treatment-price-bangalore`
