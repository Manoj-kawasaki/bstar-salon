## 5. Variance Analysis Formulas (MoM & YoY Calculation Engine)

To guarantee mathematical consistency and eliminate subjective interpretation, all monthly performance deltas are computed using standardized variance formulas.

### 5.1 Month-over-Month (MoM) Percentage Variance
$$\Delta_{\text{MoM}} = \left( \frac{\text{Current Month Value} - \text{Prior Month Value}}{\text{Prior Month Value}} \right) \times 100$$

*Example: Organic Clicks grew from 580 (Month 1) to 840 (Month 2):*
$$\Delta_{\text{MoM}} = \left( \frac{840 - 580}{580} \right) \times 100 = +44.83\%$$

### 5.2 Year-over-Year (YoY) Percentage Variance
$$\Delta_{\text{YoY}} = \left( \frac{\text{Current Month Value (Year N)} - \text{Same Month Value (Year N-1)}}{\text{Same Month Value (Year N-1)}} \right) \times 100$$

### 5.3 Average Grid Rank (AGR) Variance
*Note: In search rankings, a negative delta represents an improvement (moving closer to Rank 1).*
$$\Delta_{\text{AGR}} = \text{Current Month AGR} - \text{Prior Month AGR}$$
*If $\Delta_{\text{AGR}} < 0$, visibility has expanded. If $\Delta_{\text{AGR}} > 0$, rankings have dropped.*

*Example: AGR for "salon in tc palya" dropped from 5.2 to 4.1:*
$$\Delta_{\text{AGR}} = 4.1 - 5.2 = -1.1 \text{ (Rank Improvement)}$$

### 5.4 Net Lead Value & Return on Investment (ROI)
$$\text{Estimated Pipeline Revenue} = \sum (\text{Service Inquiries} \times \text{Average Ticket Price} \times \text{Closing Rate})$$
*Using B★STAR's empirical benchmarks:*
- Average Ticket Price (Blended): ₹1,850
- Conversational In-Salon Show-Up Rate: 42%
- Example (Month 3: 249 Inquiries): $249 \times 0.42 \times ₹1,850 = ₹193,473$ monthly attributed revenue pipeline.
